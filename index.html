About Auteur

One workspace.
One body of work.

Auteur is a screenplay and film-development workspace built around the filmmaker rather than the bureaucracy around filmmaking. Ideas can begin with a title, grow into characters and storylines, and evolve into complete films without losing the original creative thread.
