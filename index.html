About Auteur

A workspace built around authorship.

Auteur is a personal creative system for developing films and stories. The goal is simple: preserve the weirdness of the original idea while giving it enough structure to actually become something. Titles can lead. Characters can evolve. Storylines can collide. Nothing gets buried just because the project grows.
