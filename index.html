What We Do

Pre-production that starts with the story

Every great film begins long before the first frame. We help you understand, structure, and refine the narrative foundation.

📖

Story Architecture

Break down the core premise, dramatic question, and structural spine. We map acts, turning points, and emotional arcs so the story holds under pressure.

🎭

Character Systems

Build multi-dimensional characters with clear wants, needs, wounds, and contradictions. Create relationship webs and ensemble dynamics that drive the plot.

🗺️

Storyline Development

Weave A, B, and C plots into a cohesive tapestry. Identify thematic through-lines and ensure every subplot serves the central narrative engine.

📋

Production Bible

Deliver clear, actionable documents: beat sheets, character bibles, tone references, and continuity notes that directors, writers, and producers can trust.

How We Work

A clear path from idea to shoot-ready

We treat pre-production as a collaborative craft — rigorous, iterative, and always in service of the story.

01

Discovery & Diagnosis

We start by listening. What is the story trying to say? Where does it currently succeed or stall? We read existing materials, interview the creative team, and map the narrative landscape.

02

Story & Character Deep Dive

Workshops and structured analysis sessions unpack premise, theme, character arcs, and structural pressure points. We identify the strongest version of the story and the gaps that need closing.

03

Blueprint & Documentation

We produce a living production bible: refined outline, character profiles, relationship maps, tonal references, and scene-level guidance that the entire production can reference.

04

Alignment & Handoff

Final review with key creatives. We ensure directors, writers, and producers share the same understanding of the story so decisions on set stay true to the vision.

Our Framework

Tools for clarity and coherence

Practical systems we use to keep complex narratives under control.

Story

Dramatic Engine Mapping

Visual and written maps of the central conflict, stakes escalation, and the question the audience is holding. Ensures every scene either advances or complicates the engine.

Character

Want / Need / Wound Framework

Structured character profiles that go beyond surface traits. We track internal and external journeys so arcs feel earned rather than imposed.

Structure

Beat Sheet & Act Architecture

Custom structural templates adapted to genre and tone. From classical three-act to more experimental forms — always with clear turning points and emotional peaks.

Theme

Thematic Through-Line Tracking

Identify the controlling idea and ensure subplots, dialogue, and visual motifs reinforce (or productively complicate) it rather than dilute it.

Ensemble

Relationship Web Analysis

Map how every major character relates to the protagonist and to each other. Reveals missing dynamics and opportunities for richer conflict.

Output

Living Production Bible

A single source of truth: outlines, character bibles, continuity notes, and tone boards that update as the project evolves and stay accessible to the full team.

Ready to strengthen the story?

Whether you’re early in development or refining a shooting script, we help teams build narrative clarity that lasts through production.
